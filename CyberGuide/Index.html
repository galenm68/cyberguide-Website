Easy-to-understand guidance to help everyday users stay safe, secure, and confident online

Importance

Protect Your Personal Information

Your personal information is valuable. Passwords, financial details, photos, messages, and account information can all be targeted by cybercriminals. Good cybersecurity habits help keep your information private and out of the wrong hands.

Prevent Scams & Attacks

Phishing, malware, identity theft, and other online scams can affect anyone. Learning how to recognize suspicious messages, links, and websites can help you avoid becoming a victim.

Stay Safe in a Connected World

We rely on phones, computers, apps, and online accounts every day. Keeping your devices updated, using strong passwords, and enabling multi-factor authentication can reduce your risk and make your digital life more secure.

Security Checklist

Daily Habits

  • Use strong, unique passwords for your accounts.
  • Use MFA whenever it is available.
  • Think before you click links or open unexpected attachments.
  • Lock your devices when you are not using them.
  • Check what you share online before posting personal information.

Weekly Habits

  • Check for software updates on your phone, computer, and apps.
  • Review and remove unused apps or accounts.
  • Check your account activity for unfamiliar logins or transactions.
  • Back up important files and make sure your backups are working.
  • Review privacy and security settings on important accounts.
